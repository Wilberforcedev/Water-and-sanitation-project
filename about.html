About Water for All

Water is a shared foundation for wellbeing.

We are committed to practical, sustainable access to safe drinking water, shaped in partnership with the communities we serve.

Our mission

Make safe water more accessible

Water for All works to support communities facing barriers to clean and safe water. We believe solutions should be durable, locally understood and cared for over time.

Our role is to listen, work with partners and help turn shared priorities into meaningful action.

What guides us

Principles we work by

01 · Sustainability

Plan beyond the build

We value water solutions that can be maintained and supported over time.

02 · Empowerment

Put communities at the centre

We support local people to shape decisions about the water resources they rely on.

03 · Transparency

Communicate openly

We aim to be clear about our work, our partnerships and the outcomes we can verify.
