What we do

Practical solutions, shaped locally

Every community has different needs. We support approaches that make sense for the people who use and care for each water source.

⌖

Improve access

Support community water points and reliable local sources where access is limited.

◌

Protect water quality

Promote practical filtration, safe handling and awareness around clean water.

↻

Build for the long term

Encourage maintenance, shared ownership and sustainable water practices.
